How the data was assembled

The TPD figures are cross-checked against manufacturer service documentation and, where available, timegrapher observations on a winder test bench. Where a maker publishes a range rather than a single value, the repo records the midpoint and notes the tolerance. Twenty-nine calibers are covered across nine brands; anything not yet listed is either a movement we have not bench-tested or one whose maker does not publish winding requirements.

The price study is a census rather than a sample: every winder model listed for sale in the UK and EU markets in Q1 2026 whose price and slot count could be verified, normalised to cost per watch slot so that a four-slot unit and a single-slot unit can be compared on the same axis.
